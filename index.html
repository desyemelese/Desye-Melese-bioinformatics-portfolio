About Desye Melese Sisay

Desye Melese Sisay is a Senior Bioinformatician and Computational Biologist specializing in data-driven biological discovery. Expert in structural bioinformatics, biomolecular modeling, molecular dynamics, and custom pipeline engineering using Python and R. Offering end-to-end analytical services, data visualization, capacity building, and research consulting for biotech and academia.

Bioinformatics & Analytical Services

End-to-end computational biology solutions for research groups, clinical institutions, and biotech firms.

Reverse Vaccinology & Vaccine Engineering

Multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) design, recombinant antigen engineering for wild-type & escape mutant variants, immunodominant domain selection, and immune simulation.

NGS Data Analysis & Genomics

RNA-Seq differential expression, Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS), variant calling and annotation (GATK), metagenomics, and comparative phylogenomics.

Structural Modeling & MD Simulations

Protein 3D modeling (AlphaFold2/MODELLER), receptor-ligand molecular docking (AutoDock Vina), extended GROMACS MD simulations, and MM/PBSA binding free energy calculation.

Diagnostics & Biomarker Discovery

Molecular diagnostics target selection, biomarker identification, high-specificity multiplex PCR/qPCR primer design, and off-target cross-reactivity profiling.

Pipeline Automation & Machine Learning

Custom bioinformatics pipeline development using Nextflow, Snakemake, Python, and R, alongside Machine Learning models for biological feature classification.

Drug Repurposing & Target Screening

Virtual screening of small molecules, natural products, and antiparasitic compounds against receptors, channels, and metabolic enzymes.
