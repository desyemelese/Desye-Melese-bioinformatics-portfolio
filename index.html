Publications & Research Manuscripts

Peer-reviewed publications, engineered constructs, and computational modeling studies.

Vaccine Design (EHEC) Research Article

In Silico Design and Evaluation of Multi-Epitope Vaccine Candidates (MEVC) Against Enterohemorrhagic Escherichia coli

Authors: Desye Melese Sisay, et al.

Utilizing reverse vaccinology, immunoinformatics, 200 ns GROMACS molecular dynamics simulations, C-ImmSim immune profiling, and MM/PBSA binding free energy calculations to design stable vaccine constructs against pathogenic targets.

Phylogenomics Comparative Analysis

Comparative Phylogenomic and Structural Mapping of the let-7 MicroRNA Repertoire

Authors: Desye Melese Sisay, et al.

Executing evolutionary metric calculations, sequence alignment profiles, phylogenetic tree reconstruction, and miRNA-target binding stability evaluations using R scripts and miRBase databases.

Drug Discovery Virtual Screening

Virtual Screening and Computational Drug Repurposing Against Key Neuroreceptors

Authors: Desye Melese Sisay, et al.

Evaluating antiparasitic compounds and plant alkaloids against neuroreceptors (GABA-A, Nav1.7, DAT) using AutoDock Vina molecular docking and dynamic energy minimization workflows.
